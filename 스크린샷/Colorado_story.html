The Anderson Family

Winter Mountain Adventure

The Anderson family was getting ready for their winter road trip to the mountains. Every year, they took a trip to Colorado to see the snow and the mountains.

Gathering all their warm coats and cozy boots for the big trip.

They stayed in a cabin tucked away in the woods. After they packed their clothes, they were on the road! It was a 14-hour trip to Colorado, so they had a lot of driving ahead of them.

Setting off on the long highway towards the snowy peaks.

The kids, Isaiah and Makayla, kept themselves busy by playing "I Spy" out the window. Makayla even saw a black bear!

"I spy something... fuzzy!" Makayla spots a surprise guest in the trees.

Finally, after a very long car ride, the Anderson family arrived in Colorado. The snow was sparkling everywhere!

Welcome to the mountains! The air was cold and the hot cocoa was waiting.
